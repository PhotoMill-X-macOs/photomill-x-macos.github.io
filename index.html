PhotoMill X

PhotoMill X is your multi-functional batch photo converter for macOS. Effortlessly transform bulk images into popular formats, rename files intelligently, apply watermarks, adjust colors and geometry, strip private metadata, and add copyrights. It also serves as a powerful metadata editor, renamer, and photo browser—all in one streamlined app.

Batch Conversion

Convert to BMP, GIF, JPEG, JPEG 2000, PDF, PNG, TIFF, and now JPEG XL. Support RGB, Gray, CMYK color models at 8/16/32 bits, with or without alpha channel. Process thousands of files in moments.

Precise Adjustments

Refine images with scale, crop, trim, rotate, flip, auto-rotate via EXIF, and resolution changes. Apply filters like auto contrast, exposure, sharpness, grayscale, sepia, curves, HSL/HSV/RGB channels, and more. Handle transparency with alpha masks and color conversions.

Intelligent Renaming

Construct meaningful names using tags like index, capture date, original name, dimensions, and find & replace. Rename without conversion for quick organization.

Watermarking & Metadata

Add text or image watermarks with metadata tags, relative sizing, tiling, strokes, shadows. Draw rectangles with transparency. Edit EXIF/IPTC: set GPS, dates, keywords, ratings, copyrights, or remove private data.

Smart Photo Browser

Browse with Image Info, Groups (by date or criteria), and multi-criteria Search panels. Comfortably manage and explore your library.
